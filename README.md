---

I design and verify digital hardware — RTL and FSM control logic, clock-domain crossing, and
standard-cell design through DRC/LVS-clean layout — and back it with a device-level understanding
of what the silicon underneath is actually doing.

My M.Tech thesis applies deep learning to GaN HEMT compact-model parameter extraction, building a
**CNN–MOPSO hybrid framework** that replaces iterative SPICE-in-the-loop optimisation with a fast
surrogate-assisted pipeline. Supervised by Dr. Avinash Lahgere, EDCL, IIT Kanpur.
